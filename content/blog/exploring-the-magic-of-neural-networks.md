Part one of a series. A simple Celsius-to-Fahrenheit example shows the difference between traditional programming and machine learning.

## What the article covers

- **Two ways to solve the same problem.** In traditional programming you write the rule yourself. In machine learning you give example inputs and outputs, and the model learns the rule.
- **A one-neuron network.** Built with Python, TensorFlow/Keras, NumPy and Matplotlib, using mean squared error as the loss and the Adam optimizer.
- **Training and checking.** Train, plot the loss, predict new values, and inspect the learned weights, which come out close to the real formula (1.8 and 32).
- **Where it matters.** Deep learning is most valuable for complex, non-linear problems where no simple formula exists. Later parts cover the mathematics.
